Your weekly DM flow

Once entry is confirmed, use “Comment KICKSTART” in captions or “DM SPRING” in Stories. Reply by hand: Which suburb are you in, and which goal interests you? Match them to their local coach, then send the joining link: Use the same email in JF Coach, then choose your goal.

A comment isn't a registration. Record the source, suburb, link sent and confirmed registration separately, and follow up once with permission. Don't promise instant automated replies, or vacancies, prizes or late access that aren't confirmed.
