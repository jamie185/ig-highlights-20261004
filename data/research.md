# Jamie Fitness Instagram research

Checked live web: 4 October 2026. Recommendations suit @jamiefitnessau’s 3,956 followers and trainers inside Snap Fitness gyms in Melbourne and Sydney. The four-week promotion supports the existing six-week challenge, started 1 October. Account access and feature entitlements were not tested.

## 1. Story highlights

Use **seven highlights**, in this intended order: **Join, Results, Reviews, Start, Gyms, Team, FAQ**. This is a practical recommendation, not a proven Instagram ranking rule. [Later’s guide](https://later.com/blog/instagram-stories-highlights/) supports grouping campaigns, tutorials, customers and testimonials, with consistent covers; its 2019 interface instructions are dated.

| Name | What belongs inside |
|---|---|
| Join | Free Spring Kickstart, dates, who it suits, real coaches, prizes, joining link and DM invitation. |
| Results | Permissioned client stories with timeframe and context; include strength, habits and wellbeing, not just weight. |
| Reviews | Recent client quotes or videos, naming trainer and gym where permitted. |
| Start | How it works: choose Strong/Tone/Trim/Fit/Wellbeing, choose coach, snap meals, tick habits, earn points. Include a short JF Coach app recording, leaderboard and prizes. |
| Gyms | Current suburbs, Melbourne/Sydney grouping, Snap Fitness setting and trainer contact route. |
| Team | Friendly introductions, each trainer’s gym, approach and who they help. |
| FAQ | Beginners, equipment, time, app access, what “free” includes, eligibility and late joining. Confirm answers before publishing. |

Keep **5–8 useful frames per highlight** initially. Short, readable words beat emoji-only labels. “Start” houses both the app and process; avoid extra highlights that repeat it. Use consistent white or soft blue covers, one large dark icon, ample space and no tiny text. Follow Jamie’s design palette; use the JF mark only where appropriate.

Replace the two-year-old material. Refresh Join and Results weekly during October; check trainers, locations and links monthly afterwards. Remove expired offers immediately. Highlight layout and order can vary; check the actual profile after updates rather than assuming a fixed row.

**DM conversion:** Join should answer “Is this for me?” then show proof, the simple process and “DM SPRING and your suburb. We’ll help you choose a coach.” Also provide [the joining link](https://jamiefitness.au/spring-kickstart/). Reply with the relevant coach and link; track DM enquiries and completed joins, not views alone. Confirm late-entry terms before inviting October sign-ups.

**Real accounts to inspect:** [@vision_personal_training](https://www.instagram.com/vision_personal_training/) for local studios, results and app explanation; [@ultimateperformancelive](https://www.instagram.com/ultimateperformancelive/) for client evidence and locations; [@thebodycoach](https://www.instagram.com/thebodycoach/) for approachable coaching and app demonstrations. Their [official](https://www.visionpersonaltraining.com/) [websites](https://ultimateperformance.com/) [confirm](https://www.thebodycoach.com/) the accounts. Current highlight names/content could not be verified; these are reference candidates, not audited highlight examples.

Effort estimates include creation and routine upkeep. **Conditional** means check Jamie’s account before relying on it.
